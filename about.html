Geographic Excellence

With a rich heritage stemming since the 1980s, the name ‘GeoExcel’, short for Geographic Excellence, was officially brought to life in 2023.

From growing rice, sugarcane and sesame, to a single cement and steel dealership, today the GeoExcel Group has multiple verticals spanning agriculture, automotive, construction and trading.
